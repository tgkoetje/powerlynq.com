PowerlynQ Line-Card Analyzer

A personal research prototype for scoring whether published lines look complementary.

This site is a non-commercial experiment. It scores published manufacturers’ representative line cards and a candidate principal against simple fit factors: overlapping calls, technical match, conflict risk, and card load.

It does not recommend that you add or drop a line. It does not review contracts. It is not legal, compensation, or exclusivity advice. It is a first-pass screen of public information so you can see the method.

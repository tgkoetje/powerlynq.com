Complementary lines

The money is in lines that share a call—not in collecting catalogs.

A good line card lets one visit open the next. Thermal next to power electronics. Precision metals next to the assemblies they go into. Controls next to the hardware they specify. The customer already knows you. The new product has a reason to be in the room.

A poor mix does the opposite. Two factories in the same slot. A consumer line sitting next to mil-spec work. A national “we cover everything” story with two people in the truck. Principals notice. So do engineers. Complementary lines are not a slogan. They are whether Tuesday’s call is easier or harder than Monday’s.

That is what we score: the card you already run, and the factory or agency you are considering, on the same scale. Read how we ranked published cards in industrial, aerospace, medical, and electronics representation in the 2026 public line-card index.

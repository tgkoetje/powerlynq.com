Consulting offer 01 · One-pager

Line-card synergy sprint

A fixed-scope engagement for a rep firm (or a principal reviewing an agency) that needs a decision on the current book and one or two candidate pairings—not a six-month “transformation.”

Request this sprint All consulting

Who it is for

A live decision, not a study

  • Rep firm weighing a new principal against the existing card
  • Agency that suspects the current card is incoherent or overloaded
  • Manufacturer comparing current representation to a new firm

Duration

Two to three weeks

Kickoff, document and URL review, one working session, written memo. Calendar time, not a standing retainer.

What you get

Deliverables

01

Existing-book score

Coherence of the current line card or current representation on the Analyzer 0–100 scale, with named categories and coverage holes.

02

Pairing score(s)

Up to two new principals or one new firm, compared on the same scale: territory, customers, technical match, non-compete.

03

Conflict matrix

SKU- or category-level view of complementary vs colliding lines. What to ask factories in writing before LOI.

04

Add / hold / walk-away memo

A short decision document your partners or principals can read in one sitting, plus a 90-day pilot outline if you proceed.

How we work

Inputs we need from you

  • Current line card or rep list (URL or PDF)
  • Candidate principal or firm website and product/line-card URLs
  • Territory and target customer types
  • 90-minute kickoff; 60-minute readout

Out of scope

What this sprint is not

  • Not legal review of representation agreements
  • Not a full market study or account-by-account CRM audit
  • Not a promise we will take the line or appoint the firm

AI sales-workflow pilot

A short, stack-agnostic pilot that puts AI on two to four real jobs in a representative firm—research, notes, drafts—so people keep the last mile. Not a software sale.

Request this pilot All consulting

Who it is for

Agencies already dabbling, ready to systematize

  • Owner-led or multi-person rep firms using Copilot or chat ad hoc
  • Teams that will not accept unsupervised customer email
  • IT or principals who care about where data lives

Duration

Four to six weeks

Discovery, workflow map, build of 2–4 playbooks, live coaching while the team uses them on real accounts.

You pick two to four

Account briefs

Application outreach drafts

Boundaries
